# India — A Land of Dreams

From mountains high to oceans wide,
A thousand stories live inside.
With colors bright and rivers grand,
There lies my beautiful India land.

The peacock dances in the rain,
Fields of green stretch across the plain.
From village songs to city lights,
India shines through days and nights.

Diwali glows with lamps so bright,
Holi fills the air with colors and light.
With every festival, hearts unite,
Celebrating joy with all their might.

From ancient forts to cities new,
India holds a history true.
With dreams that rise and spirits free,
India grows with destiny.

O India, land of hopes and dreams,
Beautiful as the morning sunbeams.
With unity, courage, and pride,
Your future shines with every tide.
